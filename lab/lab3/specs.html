Lab 3 Specifications

Lab 3 represents the culmination of our practice with Verilog design. The specifications reflect that: the Verilog style and structure is relaxed to allow you to make more design choices, and the block diagram specs are in full force so that you effectively document your Verilog design. The specs are also strict about finite state machine format because that is a key learning goal in class.
